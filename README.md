# NexusHD

**Stream · Download · Enjoy**

NexusHD is a movie and series discovery app for your phone and the web. Find what to watch, see trailers, keep your own library, and save eligible titles to watch offline, all in one place with a dark, purple-glow look built for movie lovers.

## What you can do

- **Discover:** The Home screen shows a featured title, trending movies and browsable categories, so there is always something new to find.
- **Search:** Look up any movie, series or genre and open its details page.
- **Learn about a title:** See the description, rating, genres and trailer before you decide to watch.
- **Build your library:** Save titles to your Watchlist and sort them into Movies, Series and My List.
- **Watch offline:** Download eligible titles and watch them later without a connection. Downloads show live progress and can be removed any time.
- **Make it yours:** Switch between Light and Dark themes and turn notifications on or off.

## The screens

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Featured title, trending movies, categories |
| **Downloads** | Progress of active downloads and your saved offline titles |
| **Library** | Your movies, series and watchlist in one place |
| **Profile** | Your account, watchlist and downloads shortcuts, sign out |
| **Settings** | Theme, notifications and app information |
| **Movie details** | Description, rating, trailer and viewing options |
| **Search** | Find movies, series and genres quickly |

Navigation is a bottom bar with Home, Downloads, Profile and Library. Settings opens from the Profile screen.

## How it works

1. **Open the app** and land on Home, where featured and trending titles are ready to browse.
2. **Tap a title** to see its details and watch the trailer.
3. **Save it** to your watchlist or library so it is easy to find again.
4. **Download it** if the title is available for offline use, and follow its progress in the Downloads tab.
5. **Sign in** to keep your watchlist, preferences and download history with your account.

## Offline downloads

Downloads are only offered for titles that are cleared for it, such as public-domain or openly licensed films. Watching a trailer never gives you the right to download or share the full video.
