Questions

The short, honest answers.

Is NexusHD free?

Yes. There are no ads and no payments.

Where do the films come from?

Free Films come from the Internet Archive and are tagged by their uploaders as public domain or openly licensed. NexusHD does not host videos and does not offer pirated films. Copyright rules differ between countries, so check that a film is free where you live.

Why does Android show a warning when I install it?

Android warns about any app that does not come from the Play Store. NexusHD is signed with its own key, and each download has a checksum on the release page if you want to verify it. You only need to allow the install once.

What does NexusHD know about me?

Only what it needs: an account, if you make one, and your saved list. No ads, no trackers, no selling of data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not as an installed app. You can still open NexusHD in your browser to browse, watch trailers and play free films.
